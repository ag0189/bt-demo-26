Pillars and talking points

SC expectations at Breakthrough

1

Conduct

  • Be on time to every booth shift, session and meeting.
  • Be professional. You represent the SC team in front of customers, prospects and leadership the whole time.
2

Required attendance

  • All main stage sessions and keynotes. These aren't optional.
3

Booth duty

  • Book meetings now. Book as many booth meetings as you can through Jiffle before the event.
  • Cover open booth tasks. When you aren't booked, check the staffing sheet and fill any task that has no coverage.
  • When you're not booked, attend sessions and ask ABX if they need help.
4

Cross-functional relationships

  • Use the in-person time to build relationships with sales leadership, your sellers and the CSMs.
5

Demo expectations

Baseline

  • Existing platform. You all know it cold.
  • New releases. Be able to demo and talk to.
  • Closed Betas. Push customers to sign up. You will have to demo some of them.

6sense Labs

Labs is not a beta. It's a new program we're announcing at Breakthrough, as covered in the town hall.

  • Know the value prop. Be ready to explain Labs at a high level and show it to prospects or customers on site.
  • No new talk track required. AI Visibility (AEO) targets a persona that's new for us, so you don't need to learn a full talk track. Sheets and Data Playground are easier to pick up.
  • Built-in feedback. I'll walk you through the feedback option before the event. User feedback goes to AI coders that improve the product automatically.
Key Labs message: Labs is a new way to deliver product faster, shaped directly by user feedback.
